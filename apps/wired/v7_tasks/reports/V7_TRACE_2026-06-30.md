## 1. Headline — the misroutes are decided at **rank**, not prune

The single most important finding, uniform across the entire accuracy set:

| Router-internal fact (294 accuracy cases) | Value |
|---|---|
| `phase.rank` `router.threshold_miss` | **False on 294/294** — the router never fails its confidence floor |
| `phase.rank` `router.candidates_out` | **1 on 294/294** — exactly one winner is always elected |
| `phase.prune` `router.candidates_out` | **13–24** (never 0) — prune is a soft filter; **0 prune wipeouts** |
| `phase.prune` `router.candidates_in` | **73 on 294/294** — full registry enters prune every case |

**The router is not under-confident and it is not eliminating everything at prune.** It
confidently elects exactly one fault every time (`candidates_out=1`, `threshold_miss=False`),
and for the misroutes that one is the **wrong family**. Therefore the divergence verdict
for **every** §5 cluster and **every** dead-routing family below is the same:
**diverges at `phase.rank`** (a wrong candidate out-scores the right one) — *not* a prune
elimination, *not* a threshold miss, *not* a lab-boundary abort.

This narrows all three routing follow-ups to the **rank/scoring stage** of
`fault_atlas_router.execute_triage_loop` (the `phase.score` → `phase.rank` term weighting),
not to the prune filter or the lab emitters.

### Honest limit of the current instrumentation
The live span fields are **counts + winner** (`candidates_in/out`, `winner_id`,
`winner_score`, `threshold_miss`). They prove a winner is always elected and prune never
wipes, so divergence is *concluded* at rank. They **cannot** prove the *correct* family's
candidate survived prune into rank, because no candidate-**ID**-level span is emitted —
only survivor **counts**. Distinguishing "right candidate survived prune but lost the
rank" from "right candidate was silently dropped at prune" would need a survivor-ID span
field. That field is **not added here** (signal/noise: it has no consumer yet); it is
named as the precondition for `F-V7-DEAD-ROUTE` in §5 should the fix task need it.

## 3. Dead-routing families — phase of divergence (criterion 4)

Per [[V7_ACCURACY_2026-06-30]] §3, three families score 0 % family-routing despite
coverage. Trace verdict for all three: **diverges at `phase.rank`** — the family's
candidates are *present* through prune (13–24 survivors), but at rank a different family
always out-scores them. `threshold_miss=False` and `candidates_out=1` on every case.

| Expected family | n | `phase.prune` out (range) | `phase.rank` `threshold_miss` | `winner_id` (top, = misroute target) | Divergence verdict |
|---|--:|---|---|---|---|
| `exhaust_leak` | 10 | 16–24 | False ×10 | `SF-002` ×6 (sensor), `LC-001` ×4 (lean) | **rank** — out-scored by sensor/lean |
| `mechanical_wear` | 14 | 13–24 | False ×14 | `MF-001` ×10 (misfire), `SF-001` ×3, `LC-001` ×1 | **rank** — out-scored by misfire |
| `non_starter` | 5 | 14–19 | False ×5 | `MF-001` ×3 (misfire), `II-002` ×2 (ignition) | **rank** — out-scored by misfire/ignition |

**Read:** none of the three dies at prune (survivors 13–24, never 0) and none triggers a
threshold miss. The router *reaches* a confident decision and assigns the symptom to a
neighbouring family (mech-wear→misfire, exhaust-leak→sensor/lean, non-starter→misfire/
ignition). This is a **rank-term weighting** problem in scoring, not a missing prune path.
Whether each family's own KG candidate even survives into rank cannot be confirmed from
the count-only spans (§1 caveat) — that is the first question for `F-V7-DEAD-ROUTE`.

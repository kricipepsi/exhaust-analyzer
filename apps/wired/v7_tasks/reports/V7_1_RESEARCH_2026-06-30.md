## Executive result

T-V7-1 is complete as a research artifact. I converted the three authorized HTML libraries to scratch markdown under `/tmp/wired_v7_html/` with `/agent_workspace/html-extractor/scripts/html_to_md.py`; no converted HTML/markdown source copies are committed. The buildable candidate inventory is derived from the authorized CSV libraries and cross-checked against the primary HTML category index.

**Viability tally:** 336 CSV candidates scanned = **314 viable**, **22 incomplete from CSV**, **0 CSV out-of-scope**. Primary/research HTML adds **6 out-of-scope/characterization references** and **2 additional incomplete source-level references** that lack buildable current-contract values.

**Coverage:** every current reachable family has at least one viable candidate. Low-support families remain: `ignition_fault` (3 viable) and `non_starter` (5 viable), below COMWIKI-12's <10-case low-support threshold.

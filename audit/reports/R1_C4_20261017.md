## Approaches that found nothing

- **Enum literal sets (mechanical):** `schema_diff.py` compared 16 producer/COMLab enum pairs: ChemicalSignature 34/34, SampleValidity, the GAS and OBD engine/RPM buckets, VEValidity, TrimAnalyticsGate, NodeMAF/MAPStatus, FuelSystemStatus ×2, O2FailureMode, DTCRoutingTag 17/17, HistoricalFaultContext, DTCSeverityClass, DTCState, Mode06MonitorState↔Mode06Status. All are `SAME`. The WIRING_PREP §A 28→34 drift is closed.
- **DTCLab → COMLab models:** `DTCLabPayload`, `FreezeFrameRecord` and `Mode06MonitorResult` have 0 differences in field names, types, bounds and optionality.
- **GASLab → COMLab model:** bounds are identical. The 3 COMLab-only fields are optional and filled from raw gas input. `chemical_state` is typed `str` at COMLab, but its producer is the 34-value enum and the sets match.
- **Units and bounds on shared OBD fields:** the producer's `total_trim_correction_delta` is unbounded and COMLab's is ±254. The inputs are each bounded to ±127, so no overflow. `ve_pct` is 0-200 on both sides. Producer always-float vs consumer Optional is the safe direction.
- **DNA projection:** 25 of 27 VINLab enum values project cleanly into OBDLab, GASLab and COMLab (the remaining 2 are F-C4-7). `euro_emission_category`, `fuel_delivery_type` and `confidence` are plain `str` in GASLab, and its comparisons (`"forced"`, `.upper()`) match what the composition emits.
- **`de_cat` override not reaching GASLab:** 0 of the viable v7 rows changed `chemical_state` between `has_catalyst` True and False, so there is no proven impact (mentioned under F-C4-3 only).
- **GASLab `fuel_trim` never supplied:** the composition reads `gas_inputs["fuel_trim"]`, which neither the UI nor the corpus sets. Feeding the OBD `stft_2500_pct` instead changed 0 of 54 cases, so it is not reported.
- **"Emitter labs emit channels, not verdicts":** OBDLab and DTCLab outputs are channels and tags. OBDLab `VEValidity.FAULT_*` and GASLab `ChemicalSignature` are fault-named labels, but each lab's vocabulary sanctions them. No cross-lab verdict field was found in an emitter payload.
- **Not examined / leads for other targets:**

  - (C1) MW-002 returns `abstained=True, abstain_reason=margin` together with `is_fault_detected=True, fault_code=MW-002, confidence 0.95`.
  - (VINLab) WDD1690321J749046 (2001) and WVWZZZ1KZAP012480 (2010) decode as `EURO_1`/`HORIZON_LEGACY` with default triage.
  - `run_suite.py` scoring, `second_operating_point` (the composition never builds one), `user_input_echo_audit`, `symptom_dna`/`csv_primary_symptom` routing.
  - `load_corpus` fabricates defaults (ECT 85 °C, CL, trims 0, O2 0.45 V when absent). It is a test harness, not on the user path.
  - DNALab's own cross-lab imports (sanctioned, ADR-0002 / R0).

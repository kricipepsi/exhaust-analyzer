### F-C5-1 — A blank low-idle gas field, or an unticked GASLab box, crashes the run with `KeyError '<field>'`. This is the live "Diagnostic calculation failed: 'co2'"
severity: HIGH
kind: contract
anchor: apps/wired/wired/composition.py:169 — "co2=float(gas_inputs[\"co2\"])" (lines 166-172 index `hc, co, co2, o2, nox` unconditionally). The gas dict is therefore never empty and is always sent.
claim violated: apps/ui/ui_payload.py:825-826 — "Untouched widgets (None or empty) are omitted from the payload so downstream logic/producers can abstain on absence." Also apps/wired/ship_tasks/reports/SHIP_UI_CONTRACT_2026-07-07.md §1 — "Empty = omitted (loader semantics: blank cell → field absent → producer abstains)". Also app.py:288 — "Tick the data you'll enter today." Also COMLab_PRD.md §5.6 — "`suppress_gaslab: true` → 'Gas analyser inputs skipped because the symptom is vehicle won't start'".
repro: `P -k test_f1_`
probe: `test_f1_live_co2_message_apptest` runs the real app on the deployed `sys.path`: Tier-1 "Lack of Power", Tier-2 "Flat / sluggish always", HC 120 and CO 0.5 typed, CO₂ left blank, Run clicked. `test_f1_blank_gas_matrix` blanks each gas field in turn. `test_f1_gaslab_untick_wipes_values` fills all 5, unticks GASLab, then re-ticks it. `test_f1_non_starter_needs_gas` uses a decodable VIN, "Vehicle Won't Start" and engine state "Won't start", with GASLab unticked.

Contrast: a partial high-idle set (4 of 5) is silently ignored (`composition.py:174` `all(...)`), so low idle and high idle disagree on what "missing" means.

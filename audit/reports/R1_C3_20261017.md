## Approaches that found nothing

- **Bounds edges** (`python audit/scratch/C3/edge_sweep.py`). Every §3.1 row (RPM against the DNA rated limit ×1.05, ECT ±, load, MAP, trims ±127, IAT, TPS, voltage) accepts the boundary value and rejects a value 0.01 beyond it with `PIDValidationError`. The one exception is MAF 655.35 (F-C3-14). The bank-2 null contract holds both ways. °F, lb/min and the "canonical wins" rule convert correctly (194 °F → 90.0, 1 lb/min → 7.559).
- **Narrowband zone edges**: 0.0999 V → STUCK_LEAN, 0.10 V → λ 1.05 NO_FAULT, 0.405 V → 1.00, 0.90 V → 0.95 NO_FAULT, 0.9001 V → STUCK_RICH. Negative volts and 5 V clamp to STUCK_*. These are consistent with the code's own thresholds; the wiki-value mismatches are F-D1-2/F-D1-4.
- **Physics gates**: Gate 1 (VSS > 10 and RPM < 400) and Gate 2 (ECT ≥ 80 with "OL") raise as the wiki §4 table says. OL_DRIVE and OL_FAULT at 90 °C correctly give OPEN_LOOP without raising. That the UI offers "OL" and a warm OL input then aborts the run is wiki-sanctioned and was not pursued.
- **DTC format**: lowercase, lowercase hex, `P4xxx`, `D`, 4 and 6 characters, and leading space or newline are all rejected with `DTCFormatError`. P1xxx, P2xxx, P3xxx, B, C, U and transmission P07xx are accepted and left untagged, as §2.1 and §15 say. Whether the vocabulary's "2 = manufacturer-specific" is correct (in SAE J2012, P2 is SAE-controlled) has no behavioural effect and is not filed.
- **Ranking and mapper order stability**: all 120 permutations of a 5-code input give one (tags, rank, top3) result. The mapper's DNA branch (HIGH suppresses, PARTIAL and NONE emit) behaves as §14.2 says when it is given a view.
- **Overlapping routing patterns**: none in `routing_tag_map.yaml`. The mapper's no-break loop and the ranker's first-match `break` therefore agree today.
- **"Channels, not verdicts", OBDLab side**: `VEValidity.FAULT_*` and `O2FailureMode.STUCK_*` are vocabulary-sanctioned single-lab labels. Only DTCLab's cross-field tag (F-C3-13) asserts a cross-lab root cause.

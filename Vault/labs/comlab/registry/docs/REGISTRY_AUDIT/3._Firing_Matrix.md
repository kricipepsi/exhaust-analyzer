## 3. Firing Matrix

### 3.1 Per-Family Rule Coverage Against Curated Corpus

Based on CORPUS_AUDIT.md §5.1 per-case v1 behavior data and the rule inventory above:

### Fault Family: `lean_condition`
- **Curated Cases**: 53
- **Rules with Matching Chemistry**: 5 (LEAN_MIXTURE + LEAN_MISFIRE)
- **Rules Active (non-orphan)**: 4 (ERR-LC-001, 003, 004×2)
- **v1 Behavior**: Most cases: MISMATCH → no_fault
- **Assessment**: Only 4 rules for 53 cases; 2 are the same fault_id. Missing ERR-LC-002 (MAF contamination — the most common real-world lean cause).

### Fault Family: `misfire`

- **Cases**: 10
- **Matching rules**: 2 (ERR-CT-003, ERR-CT-004)
- **Prune reason**: ERR-CT-003 requires vvt_equipped + P0014/P0024 DTC. ERR-CT-004 is GAS_ONLY but at 0.55 confidence.

- **Cases**: (in top 5)

### 4.4 The new families — chemistry-independent

The three new fault families from `cvsgaps.md` (`vacuum_leak`, `overheating`, `charging_system`) are primarily OBD/voltage-driven rather than gas-chemistry-driven:

### Family: `vacuum_leak` (4 placeholder cases)
- **Chemistry Overlap**: Shares LEAN_MIXTURE chemistry with `lean_condition`
- **Detection Signal**: Lean trims at idle that correct at 2500 RPM (Brettschneider-confirmed)
- **v2 Strategy**: Competing rule: LEAN_MIXTURE + trim correction at 2500 RPM → vacuum_leak vs lean_condition. The RPM-dependent trim pattern is the differentiator.

### Family: `overheating` (4 placeholder cases)
- **Chemistry Overlap**: May produce NORMAL_OPERATION or HIGH_COMBUSTION_TEMP
- **Detection Signal**: High coolant temp + RPM behaviour; not always DTC-driven
- **v2 Strategy**: Primarily OBD-driven: ect_c > threshold + RPM pattern. Chemistry may be normal if overheating is mechanical (stuck thermostat), or elevated NOx if combustion-temp-driven.

### Family: `charging_system` (3 placeholder cases)
- **Chemistry Overlap**: No direct chemistry signature
- **Detection Signal**: Low/bouncing voltage, sensor noise, ghost misfires
- **v2 Strategy**: Voltage-driven: OBD voltage PID + sensor noise pattern. May trigger ghost misfire chemistry via sensor artifacts.

These families require OBD-evidence-tier rules rather than chemistry-gated rules. Their presence in the matrix is noted but their case counts are not chemistry-assigned.

## 6. Prioritized REG-V2-AUTHOR-{family} Task Order

Ordered by: (1) case volume in curated corpus, (2) chemistry coverage gap severity, (3) whether the family is new (no v1 rules exist). Each row includes a one-line justification.

### Priority: **1**
- **Task ID**: REG-V2-AUTHOR-lean_condition
- **Family**: `lean_condition`
- **Curated Cases**: 53
- **Chemistry Gap Severity**: **HIGH** — TIMING_RETARDED (~15 cases) unserved; largest family by volume
- **New Family?**: No
- **Justification**: Largest case volume + the most common chemistry (TIMING_RETARDED) has zero rules for this family. Authoring lean_condition rules first establishes the v2 evidence-tier pattern that all subsequent families follow.

### Priority: **2**
- **Task ID**: REG-V2-AUTHOR-ecu_fault
- **Family**: `ecu_fault`
- **Curated Cases**: 28
- **Chemistry Gap Severity**: **CRITICAL** — zero chemistry states, zero executable rules
- **New Family?**: No
- **Justification**: Third-largest family with zero working rules. Both KG nodes are orphans. Must be DTC-driven or OBD-driven in v2 — this is the hardest family to author and should be tackled early while context is fresh.

### Priority: **3**
- **Task ID**: REG-V2-AUTHOR-misfire
- **Family**: `misfire`
- **Curated Cases**: 44
- **Chemistry Gap Severity**: **MEDIUM** — SAMPLE_INVALID (~10 cases) + TIMING_RETARDED (~8) unserved; 30 DTCLab-crashed cases will auto-recover
- **New Family?**: No
- **Justification**: Second-largest family; 30 of 44 cases will enter the router after FIX-ENUM-1. Needs SAMPLE_INVALID-aware rules and chemistry-agnostic DTC-driven paths.

### Priority: **4**
- **Task ID**: REG-V2-AUTHOR-catalyst_failure
- **Family**: `catalyst_failure`
- **Curated Cases**: 21
- **Chemistry Gap Severity**: **HIGH** — TIMING_RETARDED (~10 cases) unserved; CATALYST_OVERWHELMED gate misses marginal degradation
- **New Family?**: No
- **Justification**: CATALYST_OVERWHELMED is a late-stage signature; v2 needs marginal-catalyst rules that fire on less severe chemistry.

### Priority: **5**
- **Task ID**: REG-V2-AUTHOR-sensor_fault
- **Family**: `sensor_fault`
- **Curated Cases**: 20
- **Chemistry Gap Severity**: **HIGH** — 4 of 6 KG nodes are orphans; TIMING_RETARDED (~8) unserved; only O2 sensor faults executable
- **New Family?**: No
- **Justification**: Needs MAF, MAP, ECT, downstream-O2 rules reactivated from orphan KG nodes. O2 perception-flag path (ERR-SF-001) is a good v2 evidence-tier template.

### Priority: **6**
- **Task ID**: REG-V2-AUTHOR-rich_mixture
- **Family**: `rich_mixture`
- **Curated Cases**: 23
- **Chemistry Gap Severity**: **LOW** — rules exist; SAMPLE_INVALID (~2 cases) + GDI-only path narrow
- **New Family?**: No
- **Justification**: Best-covered family in v1. v2 work is primarily: add TIMING_RETARDED awareness for rich→retarded overlap cases, and ensure competition groups with lean_condition for ambiguous signatures.

### Priority: **7**
- **Task ID**: REG-V2-AUTHOR-mechanical_wear
- **Family**: `mechanical_wear`
- **Curated Cases**: 11
- **Chemistry Gap Severity**: **LOW** — most chemistries covered; cross-family confusion with lean_condition
- **New Family?**: No
- **Justification**: Cross-family competition is the main v2 task (WORN_PISTON_RINGS routes to mechanical_wear but cases are labelled sensor_fault/catalyst_failure).

**Authoring dependency note:** REG-V2-SCHEMA must land before any REG-V2-AUTHOR-* task. The schema defines the evidence-tier vocabulary (definitive / suggestive / gating), competition group structure, and confidence-ceiling fields that every family authoring task references. The three new families (vacuum_leak, overheating, charging_system) are placed at positions 12-14 because (a) they have zero existing KG nodes to reference, (b) their placeholder cases need populating during authoring, and (c) the evidence-tier patterns should be proven on existing families first.

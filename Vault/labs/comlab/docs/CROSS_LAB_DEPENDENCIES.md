# 4DLab v2 — Cross-Lab Dependencies (forward registry · living table)

**Date:** 2026-06-06 · governed by `CROSS_LAB_SEQUENCING.md` (D-SEQ ratified).
Purpose: make every cross-lab obligation **visible and un-loseable**. A capability that needs more than
one lab is split into independent **emitter halves** (each lands in its lab's stage, testable against the
frozen contract) + one **fusion/consumer half** (COMLab, last). Every row is registered the moment it is
discovered, with a placeholder task in the destination lab's `tasks/` ledger.

**Gates:**
- *Stage-exit:* a stage cannot close while it owns an unresolved row (emit shipped to an existing
  consumer, or the pair registered forward).
- *Anti-orphan:* at Stage-4 close, every emitted field here must be `grep`-confirmed read by its named
  fusion consumer. A field read nowhere does not ship (signal/noise).
- *Research-first:* an emitter half marked `research-gated` is **not coder-runnable** until its lab's
  named audit questions are answered and the lab's scope decision is `revive`.

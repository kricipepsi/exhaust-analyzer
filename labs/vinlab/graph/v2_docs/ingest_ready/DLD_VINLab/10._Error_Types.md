## 10. Error Types

### Error: `VINDecodeError`
- **Base**: `ValueError`
- **Raised when**: invalid charset; checksum fail; WMI not found; year char not found; year out of scope (H2); fuel out of scope (H1)

### Error: `ChassisNumberError`
- **Base**: `VINDecodeError`
- **Raised when**: `len(vin) != 17` — likely a pre-VIN chassis/frame number

Catching `VINDecodeError` also catches `ChassisNumberError`; callers route the latter
to a legacy-identifier pipeline.

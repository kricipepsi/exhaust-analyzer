## 2. Users

Two primary personas. COMLab's behaviour does not branch on persona —
the same wizard, the same verdict shape — but the user research
informing the PRD did distinguish them.

### Persona: **Workshop technician** (primary)
- **Context**: Diagnosing a customer car, paid by the job. Needs to commit to a repair quote in under 5 minutes.
- **What they need from the result page**: Verdict + remediation steps with enough specificity to skip re-diagnosis ("replace valve stem seals — measure clearance" is good; "engine problem" is not). Runner-up panel reads as "I considered these too and ruled them out".

### Persona: **Skilled owner-operator** (secondary)
- **Context**: Diagnosing their own car. Cares more about understanding than speed.
- **What they need from the result page**: Verdict + plain-language `mechanical_failure_mode` ("what is happening inside the engine"). Power-loss percentage to estimate "is this drivable until next month". Echo audit to confirm the inputs were right.

Persona-neutral guarantees:

- The same wizard for both. No "expert mode" toggle in v1.
- The same verdict shape, including the runner-up panel and the
  remediation steps.
- The same defensive guardrails on input.
